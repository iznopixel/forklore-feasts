# Forklore Feasts

## Design direction

The site should feel very cozy, in a Nancy Meyers way: warm, lived-in, candlelit and generous. Keep it simple, but layered with character.

- Simple structure and calm layouts; let big photography and warm paper tones do the work.
- Character comes from layers and small handmade details: tape, paper cards, handwritten notes (`Annotation`), grain, flourishes, scalloped edges. Add a few, thoughtfully, never a pile.
- Warm light over cold: cream, candle-glow ambers and soft shadows. Prefer warm dark scrims to neutral black.
- Voice: warm, inviting and a little wry, like a host who has already set the table. Talk about gathering around food, RSVPing and sharing recipes, not "community" or "neighborhood cookbook". Short sentences, sensory words (candles, good bowls, still warm), no corporate or app-speak.
- Type: Young Serif for large display type, Source Sans for body, Caveat for handwritten asides.
- Palette is sampled from the star-tile reference (charcoal, maroon, olive, mustard, pink, soft blue, cream); see `src/app/globals.css`.
- The home hero is a candlelit table photo (`public/hero-feast.webp`). Text sits on a warm scrim on desktop and stacks below the photo on phones.
